# 📐 Design references

The Roadster body is modelled by hand from **public photos and video of the next-generation Tesla Roadster reveal**. Reference images are **not stored in this repository** because they're copyrighted. Keep your own copies locally in `source/reference/photos/` (git-ignored).

## Key features the model must match

### Side profile
- Very low **wedge nose**: a sharp horizontal leading edge with a black splitter lip underneath
- Front fender rises in a soft curve over the front wheel, then breaks into flat, faceted side panels
- **Long, steeply raked windshield** flowing into a glass roof (bronze/brown tint)
- Straight, gently falling roofline into a long flat rear deck
- **Black lower sill** running between the wheels, with its top edge rising toward the front
- Small amber side marker behind the front wheel
- **Gull-wing / dihedral doors** (closed in-game; shape only)

### Front
- Trapezoid front face, flat hood plane between two angular fender "blades"
- **Thin LED headlight slits** set into the inner edge of each fender blade
- Full-width horizontal light line across the nose, dark intake below

### Rear
- **Full-width red light bar** under a sharp rear lip
- **Large rear wing** on two struts. Raises in the air (animated in the mod)
- Carbon **diffuser** with **four round nozzles** (gold/bronze rims). In the mod these emit the boost
- Centre license-plate recess (the mod uses a plain plate, no real text or badges)

### Wheels
- Dark **6-spoke twisted** rims, low-profile tyres (the mod uses your equipped wheels; the stock wheel is optional)

## Rules
- No Tesla logos, "T" badges or wordmarks on the model or textures.
- No official CAD or ripped game/3D-store assets. Everything is modelled from scratch.
